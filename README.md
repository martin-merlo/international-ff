# Global Reach Redesign

You are redesigning an existing website, not creating a new business.

Current website:

https://www.internationalff.com

Analyze the existing website carefully and reuse its content, services, testimonials, contact information and overall structure. The objective is to create a premium redesign while preserving the company's identity and information.

IMPORTANT:

Do NOT invent new services or replace the existing content.

Use the information from the current website as the foundation of the redesign.

The client has already reviewed a previous design and gave the following feedback:

- "It needs more photos and images."

- "The color contrast makes the text difficult to read."

These two points are extremely important.

The new design should prioritize:

• Excellent readability

• Strong contrast between text and background

• Large professional photography throughout the website

• A modern corporate look

• Clear hierarchy

• Easy navigation

• Premium visual quality

The company specializes in international moving services, freight forwarding, international logistics and foreign trade.

The website should communicate trust, professionalism and international experience from the very first screen.

Design inspiration:

Think of the quality and elegance of websites from DHL, Maersk, FedEx or premium international logistics companies.

Website structure:

1. Sticky Navigation

- Company logo

- Home

- Services

- About Us

- Testimonials

- Contact

- Primary CTA: Request a Quote

2. Hero Section

Use a large, high-quality full-screen background image related to international logistics:

- cargo ships

- shipping containers

- air freight

- international moving

- logistics operations

Apply a dark overlay only if necessary to ensure excellent text readability.

Large headline.

Supporting paragraph using the company's existing messaging.

Primary CTA:

Request a Quote

Secondary CTA:

WhatsApp

3. Trust Metrics

Animated statistics such as:

- Years of experience

- Countries served

- Successful shipments

- Customer satisfaction

If exact numbers are unavailable, create placeholder values that can easily be replaced later.

4. Services

Reuse the services from the existing website.

Display them as premium cards with modern icons.

Each card should include an illustrative image or subtle background image.

5. How We Work

Modern four-step timeline:

Contact

Planning

Documentation

Delivery

6. Why Choose Us

Use elegant icons and concise content explaining the company's advantages.

7. Testimonials

Reuse the testimonials already available on the current website.

Display them as beautiful testimonial cards with quotation marks, customer names and ratings.

8. Call To Action

A premium section encouraging visitors to request a quote.

9. Contact

Split layout.

Left:

Company information, map and contact details.

Right:

Modern contact form.

10. Footer

Modern footer with:

- Logo

- Contact information

- Email

- Phone

- Address

- Social media

- Quick links

Design Guidelines

• Premium corporate design

• Modern typography

• Large spacing

• Rounded corners

• Soft shadows

• Smooth animations

• Fully responsive

• Navy blue, white and gold accents

• High-quality photography throughout the website

• Every section should feel visually rich without being cluttered.

• Use alternating sections with images and content to avoid long blocks of text.

• Ensure every heading and paragraph has excellent contrast against its background.

Floating WhatsApp Button

Include a floating WhatsApp button fixed in the bottom-right corner of the screen, similar to modern business websites.

The button must:

- remain visible while scrolling

- have a subtle pulse animation

- open a WhatsApp conversation when clicked

- not obstruct the content on desktop or mobile

Overall Goal

The redesign should make visitors immediately feel that they are dealing with a professional international logistics company.

The experience should feel significantly more modern than the current website while preserving the company's identity, services and content.

Do not redesign the business.

Redesign the experience.
